Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EduNova handles information when you visit the website, create an account, use learning features or purchase premium access. We aim to collect only information needed to operate and improve the service.

Important: This policy reflects the website code reviewed for this version. Some processing is carried out by third-party providers and may depend on their settings and policies. We have not confirmed that Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager is installed.

1. Information you provide

When you create or use an account, EduNova may process your username, email address, country and account-related information, including whether premium access is enabled. Authentication is provided using Supabase. Passwords and authentication credentials are handled through the authentication service; do not send your password to EduNova through feedback or email.

2. Country detection

The signup flow can request a country name from ipapi.co to help detect or prefill a visitor's country. A request to this third-party service may expose technical information such as your IP address to that provider. You may also be asked to enter or confirm your country yourself. Please review ipapi.co's website and privacy information for its handling of request data.

3. Payments

Premium payments may be processed through PayPal. Payment details you enter are handled by the payment provider under its own terms and privacy policy. EduNova's systems may receive information needed to verify a transaction and provide premium access, such as an order or payment status. We do not intend to store full payment-card numbers on EduNova's website.

4. Browser storage and similar technologies

The website uses browser storage such as localStorage for convenience, including retaining a username or email in the browser. Supabase authentication and other website features may also use browser storage or similar technologies needed to maintain a session or remember state. You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings; doing so may sign you out or affect site functionality.

5. How information is used

  • To create and maintain accounts and authenticate users.
  • To display learning features and manage premium access.
  • To process and verify payments and address purchase issues.
  • To prefill or record country information where the feature is used.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To respond to feedback and requests.

6. Service providers

EduNova uses third-party services to operate the website. These may include GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for authentication and account data, PayPal for payments, ipapi.co for country detection, jsDelivr for delivery of a JavaScript library, and Google Fonts for typography. These providers may process technical data as needed to deliver their services and are subject to their own privacy terms.

7. Analytics and advertising

We have not confirmed that EduNova uses Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager or an advertising tracking service. This policy should be updated if such a service is added or found to be active. Hosting and third-party providers may generate their own technical logs as part of operating their services.

8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the providers described above, where needed to operate EduNova, or disclosed when required by law, to protect security, or to address fraud or misuse.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may request access, correction or deletion of account information through the EduNova feedback page. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction purposes. A request may require verification of your identity.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your sign-in credentials confidential and contact us if you suspect unauthorized account use.

11. Children and students

EduNova is an educational service that may be used by students. If you are a minor, you should involve a parent or guardian when required by the rules that apply where you live, especially for purchases. Parents or guardians who have questions about a student's information may contact us through the feedback page.

12. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own. The protections and rules that apply can vary by location.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website or its providers change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was published.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the EduNova feedback page. Do not include your password or full payment-card details.
