STAGE I • DIRECT POSITION VECTORS
01
POSITION VECTOR

Find the position vector of A.

Point \(A=(7,-3)\). Find \(\overrightarrow{OA}\).

i +j
STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. The position vector starts at \(O=(0,0)\) and ends at \(A=(7,-3)\).
  2. The horizontal component is the x-coordinate, \(7\).
  3. The vertical component is the y-coordinate, \(-3\).
Final answer: \(\overrightarrow{OA}=7\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}\).
02
COORDINATES

Recover the point.

A point \(B\) has position vector \(\overrightarrow{OB}=-4\mathbf{i}+9\mathbf{j}\). What are the coordinates of \(B\)?

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. The coefficient of \(\mathbf{i}\) gives the x-coordinate: \(-4\).
  2. The coefficient of \(\mathbf{j}\) gives the y-coordinate: \(9\).
Final answer: \(B=(-4,9)\).
03
DISPLACEMENT

Find the vector from A to B.

Given \(A=(3,5)\) and \(B=(11,1)\), find \(\overrightarrow{AB}\).

i +j
STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. Subtract the coordinates of \(A\) from those of \(B\): \(B-A=(11-3,1-5)\).
  2. Calculate each component: \(11-3=8\) and \(1-5=-4\).
Final answer: \(\overrightarrow{AB}=8\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}\).
04
DISTANCE

How far apart are the points?

\(A=(-2,4)\) and \(B=(7,10)\). Find the exact distance \(AB\).

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. Horizontal difference: \(7-(-2)=9\). Vertical difference: \(10-4=6\).
  2. Use the distance formula: \(AB=\sqrt{9^2+6^2}=\sqrt{81+36}=\sqrt{117}\).
  3. Since \(117=9\times13\), \(\sqrt{117}=3\sqrt{13}\).
Final answer: \(3\sqrt{13}\) units. \(\sqrt{117}\) is also accepted as an equivalent exact value.
STAGE II • THE IDEAS START CONNECTING

The questions won't tell you what to use.

From here onward, a problem may involve more than one idea from the vectors chapter. Read carefully, choose your mathematics, and build the answer yourself.

05
MAGNITUDE

A vector is given.

Given \(\mathbf{v}=9\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j}\), find \(|\mathbf{v}|\).

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. The magnitude of \(a\mathbf{i}+b\mathbf{j}\) is \(\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\).
  2. \(|\mathbf{v}|=\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}\).
  3. \(\sqrt{225}=15\).
Final answer: \(|\mathbf{v}|=15\).
06
UNIT VECTOR

Turn the vector into a unit vector.

Find the unit vector in the direction of \(\mathbf{v}=6\mathbf{i}+8\mathbf{j}\).

i +j
STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. Magnitude: \(|\mathbf{v}|=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{100}=10\).
  2. Divide the vector by its magnitude: \(\hat{\mathbf{v}}=\frac6{10}\mathbf{i}+\frac8{10}\mathbf{j}\).
  3. Simplify the coefficients: \(\frac6{10}=\frac35\), \(\frac8{10}=\frac45\).
Final answer: \(\hat{\mathbf{v}}=\frac35\mathbf{i}+\frac45\mathbf{j}\).
07
POSITION + MAGNITUDE

A point is located in the plane.

The position vector of \(P\) is \(\overrightarrow{OP}=5\mathbf{i}-12\mathbf{j}\). Find the distance from \(O\) to \(P\).

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. The distance from the origin is the magnitude of the position vector.
  2. \(|\overrightarrow{OP}|=\sqrt{5^2+(-12)^2}=\sqrt{25+144}=\sqrt{169}\).
  3. \(\sqrt{169}=13\).
Final answer: \(OP=13\) units.
08
DIRECTION

Compare two vectors.

Consider \(\mathbf{a}=3\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{b}=6\mathbf{i}+8\mathbf{j}\). Which statement is true?

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. Notice that \(\mathbf{b}=2\mathbf{a}\).
  2. A positive scalar multiple changes magnitude but preserves direction.
  3. The magnitudes are not equal because \(|\mathbf{b}|=2|\mathbf{a}|\).
Final answer: B. They point in the same direction.
09
POSITION + DISPLACEMENT

Follow the journey.

A particle moves from \(P=(-6,4)\) to \(Q=(2,-8)\). Find the magnitude of the displacement vector \(\overrightarrow{PQ}\).

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. Displacement: \(Q-P=(2-(-6),-8-4)=(8,-12)\).
  2. Magnitude: \(\sqrt{8^2+(-12)^2}=\sqrt{64+144}=\sqrt{208}\).
  3. Simplify: \(\sqrt{208}=\sqrt{16\times13}=4\sqrt{13}\).
Final answer: \(4\sqrt{13}\) units, approximately \(14.42\).
10
VECTOR GEOMETRY

A missing vertex.

\(A=(1,2)\), \(B=(7,2)\), and \(D=(1,6)\) are three vertices of parallelogram \(ABCD\). Find the coordinates of \(C\).

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. In a parallelogram, \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{DC}\).
  2. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(7-1,2-2)=(6,0)\).
  3. Add this to \(D=(1,6)\): \(C=(1+6,6+0)=(7,6)\).
Final answer: \(C=(7,6)\).

11
MIXED

Two points, one direction.

\(A=(-3,7)\) and \(B=(9,-2)\). Find the unit vector in the direction from \(A\) to \(B\).

i +j
STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=B-A=(9-(-3),-2-7)=(12,-9)\).
  2. Magnitude: \(\sqrt{12^2+(-9)^2}=\sqrt{225}=15\).
  3. Divide each component by 15: \((12/15,-9/15)=(4/5,-3/5)\).
Final answer: \(\frac45\mathbf{i}-\frac35\mathbf{j}\).
12
MIXED

The hidden route.

A particle is at \(P=(4,-1)\). Its displacement vector is \(7\mathbf{i}+5\mathbf{j}\). What are the coordinates of its final position \(Q\)?

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. The displacement components are \((7,5)\).
  2. Add them to the starting point: \(Q=(4+7,-1+5)\).
  3. Therefore \(Q=(11,4)\).
Final answer: \(Q=(11,4)\).
13
MIXED • ANGLES

Same direction or not?

Consider \(\mathbf{u}=4\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}\) and \(\mathbf{v}=-8\mathbf{i}+6\mathbf{j}\). What is the angle between the two vectors?

STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. Observe that \(\mathbf{v}=-2\mathbf{u}\).
  2. A negative scalar multiple points in the opposite direction.
  3. Opposite directions form an angle of \(180^\circ\).
Final answer: \(180^\circ\), option C.
14
FINAL CHALLENGE

Everything comes together.

Points \(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \(\overrightarrow{OA}=3\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}\) and \(\overrightarrow{OB}=15\mathbf{i}-12\mathbf{j}\).

Find the unit vector in the direction from A to B.

i +j
STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
  1. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{OB}-\overrightarrow{OA}=(15-3,-12-4)=(12,-16)\).
  2. Magnitude: \(\sqrt{12^2+(-16)^2}=\sqrt{400}=20\).
  3. Divide each component by 20: \((12/20,-16/20)=(3/5,-4/5)\).
Final answer: \(\frac35\mathbf{i}-\frac45\mathbf{j}\).
