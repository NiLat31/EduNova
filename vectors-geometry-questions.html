01
The parallelogram connection
Warm-up

In parallelogram \(ABCD\), \(\vec{AB}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{BC}=\mathbf b\). Which expression represents \(\vec{AC}\)?

Solution

Travel from \(A\) to \(B\), then from \(B\) to \(C\): \(\vec{AC}=\vec{AB}+\vec{BC}=\boxed{\mathbf a+\mathbf b}\).

02
Read the route, not the picture
Addition

Given \(\vec{AB}=\mathbf a\), \(\vec{BC}=\mathbf b\), and \(\vec{CD}=\mathbf c\), express \(\vec{AD}\).

Solution

Follow the path \(A\to B\to C\to D\): \[ \vec{AD}=\vec{AB}+\vec{BC}+\vec{CD} =\boxed{\mathbf a+\mathbf b+\mathbf c}. \]

03
Subtraction in disguise
Subtraction

If \(\vec{AB}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{AC}=\mathbf b\), find \(\vec{BC}\).

Solution

\[ \vec{BC}=\vec{BA}+\vec{AC}=-\vec{AB}+\vec{AC} =\boxed{\mathbf b-\mathbf a}. \]

04
The midpoint test
Midpoint

\(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\). If \(\vec{AB}=2\mathbf a\), which statement is correct?

Solution

A midpoint divides \(AB\) into two equal vector parts. Therefore \(\vec{AM}=\frac12\vec{AB}=\frac12(2\mathbf a)=\boxed{\mathbf a}\).

05
Midpoint from position vectors
Midpoint

Relative to \(O\), let \(\vec{OA}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{OB}=\mathbf b\). \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\). Find \(\vec{OM}\).

Solution

The midpoint formula gives \[ \boxed{\vec{OM}=\frac12(\vec{OA}+\vec{OB}) =\frac12(\mathbf a+\mathbf b)}. \]

06
Ratio division: \(3:5\)
Ratio

Point \(P\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \(\boxed{AP:PB=3:5}\). What fraction of \(\vec{AB}\) is \(\vec{AP}\)?

Remember: the ratio \(3:5\) describes \(3\) parts from \(A\) to \(P\) and \(5\) parts from \(P\) to \(B\). The whole segment therefore has \(3+5=8\) parts.
Solution

Since \(AP:PB=3:5\), the whole segment is \(8\) parts. Therefore \[ \boxed{\vec{AP}=\frac{3}{3+5}\vec{AB}=\frac38\vec{AB}}. \]

07
Ratio with a position vector
Ratio in vector form

\(P\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \(3:5\), with \(\vec{OA}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{OB}=\mathbf b\). Find \(\vec{OP}\).

Solution

First, \[ \vec{AP}=\frac38\vec{AB} =\frac38(\mathbf b-\mathbf a). \] Then \[ \vec{OP}=\vec{OA}+\vec{AP} =\mathbf a+\frac38(\mathbf b-\mathbf a) =\boxed{\frac58\mathbf a+\frac38\mathbf b}. \]

08
Ratio from a vector expression
Reverse reasoning

A point \(P\) lies on \(AB\) and \[ \vec{AP}=\frac25\vec{AB}. \] What is the ratio \(AP:PB\)?

Solution

\(AP\) is \(\frac25\) of the whole \(AB\). The remaining part is \(\frac35\), so \(AP:PB=\frac25:\frac35=\boxed{2:3}\).

09
A parallelogram with a hidden route
Challenge

In parallelogram \(ABCD\), \(\vec{AB}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{AD}=\mathbf b\). Find \(\vec{CB}\).

Solution

In a parallelogram, \(\vec{BC}=\vec{AD}=\mathbf b\). Reversing direction gives \[ \vec{CB}=-\vec{BC}=\boxed{-\mathbf b}. \]

10
Build the expression
Challenge

\(P\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \(2:3\). Given \(\vec{OA}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{OB}=\mathbf b\), which route gives \(\vec{OP}\) before simplifying?

Solution

From \(A\) to \(P\) is \(\frac25\) of the way from \(A\) to \(B\): \[ \vec{AP}=\frac25\vec{AB} =\frac25(\mathbf b-\mathbf a). \] Starting at \(O\), go to \(A\), then to \(P\): \[ \boxed{\vec{OP}=\mathbf a+\frac25(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)}. \]

11
Ratio meets a parallelogram
Hard

In parallelogram \(ABCD\), \(\vec{AB}=\mathbf a\) and \(\vec{AD}=\mathbf b\). Point \(P\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \(3:5\). Find \(\vec{DP}\).

Solution

Since \(AP:PB=3:5\), \[ \vec{AP}=\frac38\mathbf a. \] Also \(\vec{AD}=\mathbf b\), so \[ \vec{DA}=-\mathbf b. \] Therefore \[ \vec{DP}=\vec{DA}+\vec{AP} =-\mathbf b+\frac38\mathbf a =\boxed{\frac38\mathbf a-\mathbf b}. \]

12
Final vector-geometry test
Final challenge

In parallelogram \(ABCD\), let \(\vec{AB}=\mathbf a\), \(\vec{AD}=\mathbf b\). Point \(P\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \(3:5\). Which expression is \(\vec{CP}\)?

Solution

From \(A\) to \(P\), \[ \vec{AP}=\frac38\mathbf a. \] Also \[ \vec{CA}=-\vec{AB}-\vec{AD}=-\mathbf a-\mathbf b. \] Hence \[ \vec{CP}=\vec{CA}+\vec{AP} =-\mathbf a-\mathbf b+\frac38\mathbf a =\boxed{\frac38\mathbf a-\mathbf a-\mathbf b}. \] Simplifying this expression gives the equivalent form \(\boxed{-\frac58\mathbf a-\mathbf b}\).
