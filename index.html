THE PERSON BEHIND THE PLATFORM

A passion for the ideas
behind mathematics.

Nihad Latreche is an Algerian mathematics enthusiast with a deep passion for exploring the beauty, logic and creativity behind mathematics.

EduNova was born from a simple vision: to make advanced mathematics clearer, more engaging and more meaningful for students.

Through EduNova, Nihad aims to create educational resources that encourage curiosity, logical thinking and a genuine understanding of mathematical ideas.

The journey begins with Grade 12 Advanced Mathematics, with the ambition of growing EduNova into a broader mathematical learning platform.

01 / PHILOSOPHY

Understand deeply.

Learning should go beyond remembering formulas. The goal is to understand the reasoning and connections that make mathematics work.

02 / PURPOSE

Make ideas clearer.

EduNova aims to present challenging mathematical concepts through structured explanations, meaningful examples and guided practice.

03 / APPROACH

Encourage curiosity.

Questions, exploration and logical thinking are essential parts of learning, not merely steps taken after a formula is memorized.

04 / FUTURE

Think beyond today.

What begins with Grade 12 mathematics has the potential to grow into a wider collection of resources for mathematical learning.
