Definition of Vectors.

Test your understanding of magnitude, direction, vector notation, vector addition, components, and parallel vectors.

!

Keep in mind.

  • A vector has magnitude and direction. A scalar has magnitude only.
  • Direction matters. Reversing a vector changes its direction: \(\overrightarrow{AB}\neq\overrightarrow{BA}\).
  • Equal vectors have the same magnitude and the same direction, even when they are in different positions.
  • For head-to-tail addition, the second vector starts where the first vector ends.
  • The resultant connects the starting point directly to the final point.
  • When adding components, add horizontal components together and vertical components together.
  • Parallel vectors may point in the same direction or exactly opposite directions.
  • Do not confuse magnitude with direction. Two vectors can have the same magnitude but different directions.
